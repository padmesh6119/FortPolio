Preface

Seventeen vulnerabilities. Five production platforms.

This is a reader, not a report. The findings ahead were collected between January and June of 2026 across ten production platforms — a state transport corporation, two large e-commerce storefronts, a multi-tenant college ERP, a managed cloud provider, a ride-hailing platform, and others.

Some are catastrophic. Most are not. A few are the kind of bug you read three times before you believe it: a CAPTCHA endpoint that returned the answer, a feature-flag service that handed over four hundred toggles, a session ID logged to three CDN providers on every page view.

Names are real. Payloads are sanitized. Severities are vendor-aligned. Where a finding caused real platform-wide impact during testing, it is noted. All work was performed under authorization, program scope, or responsible disclosure.

The work is the disclosure.

Every finding in this reader was reported to its respective vendor through the channel they advertised — a bug-bounty program, a security@ address, or in two cases, a written assessment with prior authorization.

One of the seventeen became a long-form write-up on Medium. The rest live here.
